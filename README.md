## Key Takeaways
- Phishing targets people, not systems, so it is often the easiest way in.
- Check the sender's domain carefully. Lookalike domains are common.
- A padlock (HTTPS) only means the connection is encrypted, not that the site is trustworthy.
- Urgency and fear are signs of manipulation. Slow down and verify through a separate channel.
- Report suspicious messages to IT or your bank right away.
